# Intake script

The questions the desk asks every lead, in this order. The same script works in a web chat or on a voice call. Scoring (`rules.md`) runs only after the last answer.

## Rules for asking

- **Ask every question, every time,** whatever the earlier answers were. Nothing ends the conversation early, and no answer disqualifies anyone mid-script.
- **Don't judge out loud.** No tier, score or "you're not a fit" during the questions.
- **"I don't know" or "I'd rather not say" is an answer.** Note it and move on. It scores as unanswered.
- **One follow-up at most per question,** only where the script gives one.
- **If they leave partway,** keep everything they answered. The desk scores it and the lead is passed on with ASK NEXT filled in.
- **Ask, don't sell.** Answer their questions briefly if they ask, then return to the script.

## The questions

| # | Ask | Scores | Follow-up (once, only if needed) |
|---|-----|--------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | "Who am I speaking with, and what's the best email to reach you?" | Contact (not scored) | If they'd rather not say yet, carry on and ask again at the end |
| 2 | "What would you like solved? Where does work break down, get repeated, or slip through the cracks?" | P | "What does that cost you today: time, money, mistakes, lost deals?" |
| 3 | "What's your role?" | R | — |
| 4 | "What's the company, and roughly how many people work there?" | S | If they give a range: "Roughly where in that range?" |
| 5 | "How many people would feel the difference if this were fixed?" | A | If they give the company size again: "And of those, how many work in the part that's broken?" |
| 6 | "When do you need it working?" | T | — |
| 7 | "What budget range are you working with: under $5k, $5–15k, $15–30k, $30–70k, or $70k and up?" | B | If they're unsure: "A ballpark is fine." |
| 8 | Close: "Thanks. We'll come back to you shortly with next steps." | — | If question 1 wasn't answered, ask for name and email now |

## Handing a conversation to the desk

When the script ends, or the person leaves, pass the whole conversation to the desk. The desk maps each answer to its question, scores per `rules.md`, and lists anything unanswered under ASK NEXT.
